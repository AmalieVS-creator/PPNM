Now for big n
The scaling of the execution time has been computed for matrix up to size n=800
It is visualized in timing.svg. 
It shows that secular function is slower for approx n<650,
but is much faster from approx n>650.
By plotting a quadratic formula it is clear,
the secular method scales quadraticly. 
While the EVD scales cubically.
